# Residential Lease Agreement (sample)

Landlord: North Star Properties Ltd.
Tenant: A. Example
Property: 12 Herzl St., Apt 4, Tel Aviv

## Term

The lease begins on 1 September 2025 and ends on 31 August 2026. The tenant
may extend for one additional year by giving written notice **no later than
60 days before the end date** (i.e., by 2 July 2026).

## Rent and deposit

Monthly rent: 6,400 NIS, due on the 1st. Security deposit: 12,800 NIS,
returned within 30 days of move-out less documented damages.

## Early termination

The tenant may terminate early with 90 days' written notice and by presenting
a replacement tenant acceptable to the landlord.

## Repairs

The landlord is responsible for repairs above 350 NIS; the tenant covers
minor upkeep below that amount. Water heater and air conditioning are under
the landlord's responsibility in all cases.
