# Couple Lab application shell

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Audience and job:** two partners at one computer who want to begin and complete a guided conversation with minimal interaction.
- **Primary action:** begin the next useful conversation; during practice, keep the camera and question visible together.
- **Content hierarchy:** couple identity and next action first; recent reflection and rhythm second; assessment, guide, report, privacy and diagnostics remain accessible but secondary.
- **Direction:** quiet editorial intimacy — warm mineral ground, deep forest workspace, restrained clay and honey accents, portraits, open sections and fine dividers instead of dashboard-card mosaics.
- **Memorable moment:** the home becomes one dark, calm invitation into the next conversation; practice gives the live camera the largest surface.
- **Constraints:** Hebrew RTL, Windows desktop first, usable mobile web, local privacy, no clinical certainty, no scores during recording, preserve all existing data and behavior.
